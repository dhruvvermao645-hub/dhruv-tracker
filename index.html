Kegels — the honest version

Kegels do not increase size — nothing exercise-based does, and skip anything claiming to (jelqing, pumps, pills = injury risk, zero evidence).

What they DO, with real research behind it: stronger pelvic floor → firmer erections and better control/lasting longer. Worth 3 minutes a day.

  • Find the muscle: the one that stops urine flow. Test once to locate, never as a habit mid-pee.
  • Squeeze 3–5 sec, release fully — ×10 = 1 set.
  • 3 sets/day at your desk. Invisible. Breathe normally, don't clench abs/thighs.
