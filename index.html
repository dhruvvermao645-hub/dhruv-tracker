The arc — Aug → Feb in four phases
  • Phase 1 · 3 Aug – 27 Sep (wk 1–8): base habits, gym split, skin/hair basics. Result: gym habit built, weight flat, evenings missed.
  • Phase 2 · 28 Sep – 30 Nov (wk 9–17): evening half fixed, cardio/core, corrected skin, serum, learn block, calorie steps. Derm visit by 15 Oct. Bloods 1–7 Nov.
  • Phase 3 · 1 Dec – 31 Jan (wk 18–26): winter arc proper. Gym exercise swaps (new stimulus), warm-up mandatory, cream moisturiser, AQI rule for outdoor walks, late-sun rule. Weight target 60–61 kg by 31 Jan.
  • Phase 4 · 1 – 28 Feb (wk 27–30): consolidate. Feb bloods only if Nov flagged something. Photos 1 Feb + 28 Feb vs 1 Aug. Decide the summer plan from data, not feelings.
  • Deload weeks — 14, 20, 26 (26 Oct, 7 Dec, 18 Jan): half the sets, same weights, no rope. Not optional — a 56-kg beginner on 6 days/week without deloads stalls or gets injured by January.
Gym — full review + what changed

Verdict: the split (Push/Pull/Legs ×2, 6 days) is right for the goal and the exercise selection is sound — compound first, isolation after, laterals high-rep, rows varied. Four real gaps, all fixed:

  • No warm-up existed. Now the first row of every gym card: 3 min rope/bike + 2 ramp sets of the first lift (50% then 75% of working weight, 5 reps each). In Delhi winter with cold joints this is the difference between a strain and a session.
  • No deload. Weeks 14, 20, 26 as above.
  • No progression past week 8. Same 6 workouts for 7 months stops working around week 12–14. Phase 3 (1 Dec) swaps one or two movements per day for the same pattern — the tracker switches automatically and the set logger keeps separate history for the new names.
  • Two grip items back to back (hanging knee raise + dead hang on Push A) — moved dead hang after the ab work, and dead hang now progresses: 20–30 s (P2) → 45 s (P3) → 60 s (Feb).

Winter arc — what Delhi Nov–Feb changes
  • Air. Nov–Jan AQI in Delhi is regularly 300+. Rule: AQI above 200 (check an AQI app before the 9 pm walk) → walk becomes 12 min of stairs indoors. Rope is already indoors. Morning gym commute: mask on if you walk there.
  • Sun. Sunrise is after 7 in Dec–Jan and the 6:45 slot is dark and cold. From 1 Nov the sunlight item moves to 8:45 after gym — 10 min of real sun on face and forearms. D3+K2 becomes more important, not less.
  • Skin. Pond's gel is a summer moisturiser. From 1 Nov: cream (Cetaphil / Venusia / any plain cream, no fragrance) at night; gel can stay under SPF in the morning. Salicylic drops to 2 nights (Mon/Fri) in Dec–Jan if the face feels tight; retinal eye cream stays Tue/Thu. Lips: plain petroleum jelly at night. Hot showers dry the skin — lukewarm only.
  • Hair. Daily gym-day washing continues (sweat is sweat). Lukewarm, never hot. Dandruff typically worsens in Delhi winter — if it flares, KZ goes to Mon/Wed/Fri for 3 weeks, then back to 2.
  • Food — winter is the easiest time to gain. Add-ins that fit the plan: peanuts 30 g as a second snack, til-gud only 1 small piece, sarson saag + makki/bajra roti (iron), gajar (not gajar halwa daily), hot haldi milk, dates. Water: 3 L still — you drink less when cold, tick it anyway. Weight steps continue: not up on a Sunday → next step.
  • Body. Warm-up is now a tracked row. Yoga in a warm room, socks on. Sleep with the phone away is easier in winter — use it.
  • Mood. Less light + WFH + winter = flat mood for many people. The walk, the sun slot and the learn block are the three that protect it. Low for 2+ weeks → doctor, same as the rule already in Track.

Acne + dark spots — the plan and the honest limit
  • What the routine can do: salicylic ×3 nights + Perobar wash-off Sat + SPF + vitamin C mornings = fewer new bumps, slower fading. 8–12 weeks of consistent nights before judging.
  • What it can't: clear what's in your photos quickly. That needs a prescription. Book a different dermatologist by 15 Oct — a government hospital skin OPD in Delhi (Safdarjung / RML / AIIMS) is cheap and sells nothing. One visit covers acne AND temples.
  • Hygiene, daily: shower within 20 min of gym · headband · pillowcase Wed + Sun · phone wiped · no picking.
  • Whey: not cut now. If PM skin is done ≥25/28 nights by 25 Oct and acne is unchanged → 3-week no-whey test (100 g paneer + extra dal instead).
  • Creatine — no good evidence it causes acne. Stays.
Hair — serum, dandruff, what actually moves the needle
  • Recapro nightly + 4-min scalp massage. Morr Pro optional AM to finish the bottle (both Procapil). Judge with the 1st-of-month crown photo, Nov → Feb.
  • Temples in a man in his 20s is usually pattern loss. Minoxidil (OTC) and finasteride (prescription) are the two with strong evidence. Ask the derm directly: "is this androgenetic, should I be on minoxidil?"
  • Dandruff: KZ Mon/Thu, full 5-min contact. Oiling paused till 25 Oct; verdict then. Rosemary oil (diluted, temples, Saturday, washed out same day) can return after the verdict.
  • Traditional remedies with any evidence: daily scalp massage (Japan) ✓ · rosemary oil ✓ · pumpkin seeds oral (weak) · green tea. Avoid: He Shou Wu / Fo-Ti (liver), unlabeled churnas, onion juice (wrong type of hair loss).

Sexual performance — control + stamina

Your two problems: finishing too fast and running out of energy.

  • Stop-start practice, Tue/Thu/Sat ("Control practice" row): build to ~7/10, stop completely, 5 slow reverse-kegel breaths (belly out, floor loose), restart. 3–4 cycles, finish only on the last. 6–8 weeks for most men. The skill is noticing 7/10 early.
  • Reverse kegels matter more than kegels for you. Clenching speeds it up. Kegels build the brake; reverse kegels keep the floor relaxed during. Both in the desk block.
  • Energy = conditioning + calories. Rope, walk, extra PB, weight going up. Expect change in 4–6 weeks as the rope gets easy. Ferritin in Nov rules out low iron.
  • Sleep 7.5 hr, phone away. Porn: if frequent, 6 weeks off is one of the best-tested fixes.
  • Nov bloods: morning total testosterone (8–10 am, fasting).
  • Skip: Tentex, Himcolin, stamina churnas, numbing sprays, sildenafil/tadalafil without a doctor. Size does not change with anything.

Still a problem by Nov → same doctor visit as the bloods. Common, treatable.
